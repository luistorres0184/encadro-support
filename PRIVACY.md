# Privacy Policy — Encadro

**Last updated:** 8 August 2026

## The short version

Encadro collects nothing. It has no network code, no analytics, no accounts, and
no third-party SDKs. Your videos never leave your Mac.

## What Encadro does with your files

**Your recordings.** When you open a video, Encadro copies it into its own
sandboxed container so it can read the file reliably while you work. That copy
lives on your Mac and is deleted when the project closes. The original file is
never modified.

**Your exports.** Rendered videos are written only to the location you choose in
the save dialog.

**Your saved templates.** Looks you save are stored on your Mac at
`~/Library/Containers/com.luistorres.Encadro/Data/Library/Application Support/Encadro/`,
as a plain JSON file plus any background images you picked. Deleting a template
deletes its image. Deleting the app removes all of it.

All video decoding, compositing and encoding happens locally, on your machine.

## What Encadro does not do

- It does not connect to the internet. There is no networking code in the app.
- It does not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports or usage statistics.
- It does not require an account, and there is nothing to sign in to.
- It does not include advertising or third-party tracking libraries.
- It does not read files you have not explicitly opened.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose — to
anyone, including us.

## Permissions

Encadro runs inside the macOS App Sandbox. It can only read files you choose
through the open dialog or drag onto the window, and can only write to the
location you pick when exporting. It requests no other permissions — no camera,
no microphone, no screen recording, no contacts, no location.

## Children

Encadro is a video tool for general audiences and collects no personal
information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with
a new date at the top. Since the app collects no data, any change is likely to be
a clarification rather than a change in practice.
